How to use the Lab / what to select

Prediction → builder
1 — Pick the exact product

Match the bookmaker screen to GT eFootball, eAdriatic, SRL, Virtual Football or Zoom/Turbo. Never mix their statistics.

2 — Pick one market

Use the exact market and line shown by the bookmaker. A signal for 1X2 is not a signal for a different market or total line.

3 — Wait for a research-qualified candidate

The engine must have enough settled history, an untouched test period, stable results and positive paper economics. Raw win rate alone never qualifies.

4 — Match today's price

A historical signal can disappear at a worse price. Current odds must satisfy the candidate's validated price condition.

5 — Select only the displayed candidate

When the Lab shows RESEARCH-QUALIFIED, the card identifies the exact product, market and selection. Otherwise the instruction is NO SIGNAL.

6 — One daily paper signal

The workflow is capped at one paper signal per day. There is no automatic wagering or forced selection.
