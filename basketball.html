How to read this — one clear decision

Model projected total = our estimate of combined points. SportyBet total line = the number your Over/Under is settled against. If the model is 226 and the bookmaker line is 221.5, the model leans Over by 4.5 points; if it is 217, it leans Under by 4.5. A lean is research, not a guaranteed or qualified bet.

Over wins when both teams’ final points added together finish above the line. Under wins below it. If the total lands exactly on a whole-number line, the market may push/refund. NBA totals here include overtime.

Model at least 3 points above line → Over lean Model at least 3 points below line → Under lean Less than 3 points difference → No clear lean
