HOW QUALIFICATION WORKS

Three gates before a batch earns its place

Reference guide · Does not override the engine
01

Match the quote

The selection must match the exact SportyBet market, line and side, with a recent timestamp. A changed or stale price blocks qualification.

02

Pass the evidence gate

Model probability alone is not proof. The candidate must meet its own product, participant-evidence and market-specific validation conditions.

03

Validate the whole ticket

Leg count, combined odds, timing, correlation and expected ticket value are checked together. The engine must not add weak legs just to reach a target.
